## The short version

GlobalLogistics is a 350-person European customs & trade-compliance firm. They clear 500,000+ customs declarations a year, almost entirely by hand, and they want to transform with GenAI — moving their people *"from typists to advisors"*. **You've been brought in as the Engagement Manager to lead that transformation.**

The full brief — context, the strategic challenge, stakeholders and your assignment — is in:

## How to approach it

This is **deliberately messy** — exactly like the start of a real engagement. A few things worth knowing:

- **You are not expected to read all 500 documents, or every Excel row, by hand.** Part of what we're interested in is *how you'd use GenAI* to ingest, structure and make sense of this kind of raw input — extraction, OCR/vision for the scans, classification, reconciliation, summarisation.
- The interviews **corroborate and sometimes contradict** each other — and the data. Triangulating the real story is part of the exercise.
- A **small working proof-of-concept on a sample** (a prompt, a CustomGPT, a worked example on a handful of documents) is very welcome — more useful to us than a perfect slide deck.

## What to deliver

Whatever format works best for you — a written document, slides, a walkthrough, a working prompt/GPT, or a combination. We care about your **reasoning**, how you **structure ambiguity**, and how you **handle messy real-world data** — not presentation polish. There are no "right answers".

Good luck — we're looking forward to seeing how you think.
